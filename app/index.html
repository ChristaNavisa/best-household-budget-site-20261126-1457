Dashboard Budget Transactions Bills Goals Reports Calculator

HearthLedger is a household budget planner built for one family’s real month: $6,400 take-home pay, $5,213 spent so far in November, $900 saved. Figures are the family’s own plan; anything you change is stored only in your browser.

Method: plan first, spend second. Needs, wants and savings are split before the month starts, bills are ticked off as they are paid, and the buffer — not a credit card — absorbs surprises.
